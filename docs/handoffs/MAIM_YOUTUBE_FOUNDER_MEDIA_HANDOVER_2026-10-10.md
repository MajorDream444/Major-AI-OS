## Executive brief
MAIM (Major AI Mindset) is a lifelong learning and development ecosystem connecting generations, cultures, technologies and economic opportunity—not merely an AI course, a storefront, or a YouTube channel. The YouTube channel is a public narrative and learning interface for the founder's journey and the evolving MAIM curriculum. **Major Dream Williams is the original blueprint, not a person to be cloned into every learner.** The goal is to extract lived experience, worldview, decision heuristics, values and experiments; convert these into teachable, falsifiable and revisable quests and assessments; learn from consented participant feedback; then publish framework V2/V3/V4.

**Signature:** “The most future-proof technology you will ever develop is your own mindset.”
**Positioning:** “Timeless wisdom. Emerging technology. An evolving mindset.”
**Ethos:** curiosity, adaptability, context, belief, experimentation, capability with character, stewardship and generational legacy.
