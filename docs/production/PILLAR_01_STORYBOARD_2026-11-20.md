## Three decisions this document assumes

State disagreement before Chapter 2 renders; each is expensive to reverse
at scale.

### 1. Chapters are authored clean; the headline is burned at export

**Assumed: author clean, burn late.**

In the approved Chapter 1, the headline lockup is still full-screen at
9.5s. Six chapters each ending on a held headline will assemble into six
posters in a row, not one continuous film — which contradicts the
requirement that assembled pillar films flow without restarting the logo
or music every ten seconds.

So each chapter renders with a **clean out-frame**. The headline lockup,
champion stripe and medallion are an **export-time overlay** applied only
to the standalone social cut. The assembly version never receives them.

This keeps one render per chapter and makes the lockup a reusable
component, which the production recipe's archive step already calls for.

### 2. The −16 LUFS master is a bed spec, not the final standard

−16.2 LUFS with 3.8 LU of range and no voice is an **ambient bed that
happens to land on target**. It is not the film's final mix.

When Major's voice goes in it becomes the loudness anchor, the bed drops
12–18 dB beneath it, and the integrated target is re-struck around the
voice. **Do not lock −16 LUFS as the brand standard from these files.**
Lock it after one chapter carries real narration, or all sixty chapters
get re-mastered later.

### 3. Title gold vs caption highlight — unresolved, needs Major

`docs/production/SUBTITLE_STYLE_RULES.md` (approved 26 Oct 2026) sets the
active-word caption highlight for **Major AI Mindset** as **blue or
orange**, reserving **gold** for MAIM Academy.

The Chapter 1 headline highlights "Distance" in **gold**.

A title lockup is arguably not a subtitle, so the rule may not bind it.
But once narration captions burn in, a gold title and blue/orange
active-word captions will share the frame. Decide deliberately:

- **A** — titles stay gold, captions go blue or orange per the standard.
  Two accent colours on screen; the stripe already carries all three.
- **B** — titles adopt the channel's caption accent, dropping gold here.
  Loses the strongest brand colour from the title.
- **C** — amend the subtitle rules to cover titles explicitly.

Recommendation: **A**, with the caption accent fixed per collection and
held across all sixty chapters. But this is a brand call, not a technical
one.

## Standing rules for every chapter

**Timing shape.** Roughly 1.5s to establish, 5.5s for the single move,
3s to land. One thought per chapter. Nothing hurries.

**Camera.** Slow and continuous. No cuts inside a chapter — the Living
Islands world holds because the camera behaves consistently. Movement is
drift, rise, or widen; never a whip or a snap.

**Continuity.** The home island is the same island in every chapter, from
Chapter 1's established geometry. The gold thread is the only element
permitted to change colour temperature. Dusk holds throughout; no chapter
moves to day.

**Type.** Mono eyebrow `PILLAR 01 · DIASPORA EMPOWERMENT` on every
standalone cut. Headline maximum three lines vertical, two horizontal.
Champion stripe (green / gold / blue) beneath. Medallion small,
bottom-right, never distorted or recoloured — Crown Rule.

**Safe area.** The stripe and medallion sit in the bottom ~20% of the
vertical frame, where Reels and TikTok place caption text and action
buttons. **Verify against real platform UI overlays before Chapter 2
renders.** If it clips, the lockup moves up once, here, not sixty times.

**Audio.** Bed only until narration exists. Bed continues unbroken across
chapter boundaries in the assembled cut, so each chapter's bed must be
renderable as a continuous stem, not a self-contained 10s loop with its
own fade in and out.

## The six chapters

Narration lines below are **drafts to be rewritten**. Ten seconds carries
roughly 20–25 spoken words. Chapter 2 is Major's own life and should be
his words, not a writer's approximation of them.

### Chapter 1 — Distance *(approved, locked)*

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Beat | Distance doesn't have to mean disconnection |
| Frame | Home island, lit and warm. Distant city, cool. One gold thread between them |
| Camera | Slow drift, holding both islands in frame |
| Narration | *"Distance doesn't have to mean disconnection."* |
| Out-frame | Clean — thread established, both islands held |

Locked as the visual benchmark. Chapters 2–6 match its lighting, scale
and camera behaviour.

### Chapter 2 — What travels with you

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Beat | You did not arrive empty |
| Frame | Close on a figure arriving at the city island. Around them, soft lights rise — knowledge, culture, relationships, craft. Each light a warm tone drawn from the home island's palette |
| Camera | Slow push in, then settle |
| Narration draft | *"You didn't arrive empty. You brought the whole island with you."* |
| Out-frame | Lights held around the figure, unremarked |

The lights must read as *carried*, not granted. They rise from the
figure, not down onto them.

### Chapter 3 — What gets overlooked

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Beat | Capability is already here, scattered and uncounted |
| Frame | Pull wide. Dozens of separate lights across many islands and city blocks — each one a person. None connected. The gold thread from Chapter 1 is one line in a field of unlinked points |
| Camera | Steady rise, widening |
| Narration draft | *"Nobody's counting what you carry. Not yet."* |
| Out-frame | Field of unconnected lights, held |

This is the chapter that earns the rest. Hold on the scattering long
enough to feel wasteful.

### Chapter 4 — Connection

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Beat | Someone recognises what another already knows |
| Frame | Two lights in the wide field brighten. A thread forms between them — fast, then settling. Then a third joins. Not a network diagram: three people |
| Camera | Slow move in toward the forming connection |
| Narration draft | *"Then somebody needs exactly what you already know."* |
| Out-frame | Three linked lights, the rest of the field still dark |

Restraint matters. If the whole field lights at once this becomes a
technology advert instead of a human one.

### Chapter 5 — Circulation

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Beat | It moves both ways, and always did |
| Frame | Light travels the thread from city back to the home island — then outward again from home to a third place. The home island brightens from receiving, not only from sending |
| Camera | Gentle lateral drift following the movement |
| Narration draft | *"And it goes both ways. It always went both ways."* |
| Out-frame | Light mid-travel, motion continuing past the cut |

The reversal is the point of the whole pillar. Home is not only a place
people leave.

### Chapter 6 — Possibility

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Beat | An open question, not a conclusion |
| Frame | Widest view of the film. Many threads now, but deliberately not all — clear dark space remains between lit clusters |
| Camera | Continuous widen, slowing but not stopping at the end |
| Narration draft | *"What could you help reconnect?"* |
| Out-frame | Still widening. The film does not resolve to a logo |

The unlit space is intentional and should survive review. A fully
connected final frame says the work is finished; it isn't.

## Series opener (20–30s)

Precedes all ten pillar films. Shares the Living Islands world.

**Visual.** Open close on the familiar home island. Camera rises and
widens continuously as ten distinct places emerge within one shared
world — each a pillar, each visually its own but built from the same
materials. Threads move between them. End on the whole world held, with
room still visible around it.

**Narration draft** — Codex's version, which reads well and needs only
Major's cadence:

> "AI keeps changing. How do you stay grounded while the world moves?
> The Ten Pillars of the Major AI Mindset bring us back to people,
> culture, ownership, wellbeing, and legacy. Ten connected principles.
> Different places to begin. What needs your attention today?"

Ending on a question rather than a claim preserves the principle that
people enter through whatever question they hold that day.

**Note.** Ten visually distinct places inside one coherent world is the
hardest asset in the collection and gates the other nine films' look.
Build it early, not last.
