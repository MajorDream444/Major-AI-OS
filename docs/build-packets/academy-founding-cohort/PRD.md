It still contains legacy session dates. Use the later founder schedule for reconciliation.

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AFC-01 | Show a consistent offer, schedule, capacity and availability. | Public page, invitation, checkout, terms and calendar agree with one approved version. |
| AFC-02 | Review readiness before inviting payment; hold offered seats for 72 hours. | Decision within two working days and one traceable seat hold per approved applicant. |
| AFC-03 | Confirm payment and grant the correct access. | A verified payment creates one order and correct entitlement; test the cap, replay and refund. |
| AFC-04 | Deliver orientation, four Sunday group sessions, Wednesday office hours, a 45-minute 1:1 and six project reviews. | Learner can access approved sessions and assignments; replays within 24 hours and feedback within three working days. |
| AFC-05 | Review demonstrated work and manage workspace access. | Six projects have peer-first facilitator receipts, attendance or replay takeaway and showcase evidence where credentialing applies. Test 12-month workspace expiry and 30-day export warning before promising them. |
