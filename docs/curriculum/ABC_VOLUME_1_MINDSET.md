# MAIM ABCs — Volume 1: Mindset and philosophy

**Founder supplied:** 1 November 2026 (Asia/Makassar). **Status:** selected working A–Z map for curriculum and site planning. A–E already appear in the Major-AI-OS README and existing lessons; F–Z came from a previously proposed list that Major subsequently supplied as Volume 1. This map records the supplied wording; it does not certify that all 26 lessons, books, quests, or products are complete.

Major AI Mindset helps people see, decide, and grow. The ABCs are an A–Z learning framework, not a five-letter starter product or a rigid sequence. Learners may revisit letters as their circumstances change.

| Letter | Concept | Guiding question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Awareness | What is happening in my life and work? |
| B | Belief | What do I think is possible? |
| C | Context | What shaped this situation? |
| D | Direction | Where am I choosing to go? |
| E | Experiment | What can I try and learn from? |
| F | Focus | What deserves my attention now? |
| G | Goals | What outcome am I working toward? |
| H | Habits | What do I practice consistently? |
| I | Identity | Who am I becoming? |
| J | Judgment | How do I make better decisions? |
| K | Knowledge | What do I need to understand? |
| L | Legacy | What will outlast my immediate success? |
| M | Mindset | How do I respond when things change? |
| N | Network | Who can I learn and build with? |
| O | Ownership | What can I create and control? |

## Editorial and product use

“Explore the ABCs” can be the central site doorway to all 26 letters. Each letter may develop into a lesson, founder story, exercise, and real-world quest after author and learning review. Books, workbooks, courses, and coaching can deepen the framework; their availability, prices, access and delivery must be verified separately. Do not turn this map into 26 product listings or claim finished curriculum.

**Companion:** [MAIM Academy ABCs — Volume 2](https://github.com/MajorDream444/MAIMACADEMY/blob/docs/academy-abc-volume-2-map/docs/curriculum/ABC_VOLUME_2_ACADEMY.md) is the applied build-and-do pathway. Academy artifacts and assignments are distinct from Volume 1 reflection.
