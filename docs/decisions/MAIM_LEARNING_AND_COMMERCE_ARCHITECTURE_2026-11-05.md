# MAIM Learning and Commerce Architecture

**Decision date:** 2026-11-05  
**Status:** FOUNDER-APPROVED  
**Authority:** This repository owns the MAIM philosophy, doctrine, learning journey, and ecosystem meaning. It does not own individual Stripe objects or release readiness.

## Core distinction

- **MAIM develops the mind.** It teaches people how to think before they build.
- **MAIM Academy develops the practice.** It teaches people how to act, build, test, and execute.
- **Spark Lab converts practice into proof.** It provides facilitated 7-, 14-, 21-, and 30-day work sprints with bounded outcomes.

These layers are connected, but they are not interchangeable.

## Learning architecture

### ABCs Volume 1 — MAIM

Volume 1 establishes the philosophy: awareness, belief, context, direction, and the other thinking disciplines required before execution. The ABCs are recursive, not a simple linear checklist.

### ABCs Volume 2 — MAIM Academy

Volume 2 translates philosophy into action through workflows, operating models, agents, harnesses, projects, and repeatable execution systems.

## Customer paths

1. **Philosophy-first:** free MAIM material → Volume 1 → MAIM Foundations Cohort → Academy → Spark Lab → portfolio and leadership.
2. **Direct Academy:** experienced builders may enter the Academy directly when they already understand their problem and intended outcome.
3. **Institutional:** schools, communities, sponsors, and organizations may buy licenses, facilitator kits, sponsored seats, or deployments.

For participants under 18, the payer and contracting party must be a parent, guardian, school, or sponsor.

## Approved offer families

### MAIM philosophy products

Books, the Ten Pillars, journals, cards, diagnostics, workbooks, guides, audiobooks, medallions, pendants, and outcome-based bundles.

### MAIM Foundations Cohort

Guided thinking, problem diagnosis, context formation, and a personal or business system map.

### Academy products

ABCs Volume 2, courses, the six-project pathway, automation and agent labs, templates, office hours, feedback, portfolio review, cohort tuition, and a recurring Campus membership.

### Spark Lab

Facilitated fixed-outcome sprints, including Business Pressure Test, Build While You Heal, Stop the Bleed, Wolf Protocol, and future approved sprints.

### Institutional

School and community licenses, facilitator kits, sponsored seats, and structured deployments.

### Physical and collectible objects

Medallions, pendants, pillar coins, pins, and completion objects. These must carry meaning inside the learning system; they are not generic merchandise.

### Recurring layer

The Academy Campus Pass may provide bounded recurring entitlements. It must not promise unlimited human labor.

## Commerce operating rule

Every commercial item must be represented in the MAIM Commerce Catalog before release. The catalog record must identify the outcome, audience, payer, product family, SKU, price model, Stripe mapping, entitlement, fulfillment method, legal version, dependencies, owner, evidence, and release status.

The catalog is the operational source for offers. This document remains the authority for what the ecosystem means.

## Release boundary

A concept, completed asset, merged branch, Stripe object, and verified live offer are different states. No offer is “live” until checkout, payment, webhook, persistence, confirmation, fulfillment, and customer access are verified in the intended environment.
