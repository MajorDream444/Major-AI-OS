## Governance
Do not present personal anecdotes as verified psychological science. Separate normative principles from empirical claims. No gender stereotyping, coercive relationship advice, diagnosis, or therapeutic credential claims. Distinguish legitimate privacy from deception. Protect identities in source conversations and obtain consent for publication.
