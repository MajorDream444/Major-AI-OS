How wisdom moves.

Likkle by likkle. Every turn of this loop adds more wisdom, more proof, and more people sharing what they know.

  1. FindA conversation, or a review of a business, surfaces what you know that nobody can Google.
  2. CaptureA Pathfinder sits with you and records it: the stories, the steps, the scripts, the red flags, the "never do this."
  3. ShapeIt becomes a Wisdom Unit — structured, reviewed with you, tested to show it actually helps, and licensed on your terms.
  4. RunHanzo hosts it so agents can call on it, and counts every use. Your wisdom stays protected in an owned cloud.
  5. ReturnRoyalties go back to the person who shared it, and to the Pathfinder who captured it.
